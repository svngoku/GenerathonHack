# Visual bible — Jua

Most of this film is **real**: the print, the hands, the voice. AI (in Arcads, prompted as Jua's pipeline) touches only the staged restoration and one restrained motion shot. That ratio is the point.

## Look
| Beat | Light | Palette | Texture |
|---|---|---|---|
| Absence (01–02) | cool, flat daylight / screen glow | grey, paper-cream, cyan UI | dust, scratches, silence |
| Curiosity (03) | one window-light shift crosses the photo | warming grey → honey | slow, still |
| Belonging (04–06) | late warm daylight on a wooden table | honey #D9A35F, paper #F4EAD5, ink blue | hands, paper grain, real voices |

- Camera: static or one slow push per shot. Top-down for hands and table. Macro for the print.
- Never show the restored image without the original nearby in the same beat (02, 06).
- Type: one humble serif for subtitles and end card; no generated lettering. The name on the sleeve is **real handwriting**.
