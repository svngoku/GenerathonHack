# Jua v13: the forgotten box (Marty's restructure, 27 Sept)

**Arc:** absence → curiosity → **joy** → belonging.

Across Africa, families keep very few photographs. Marty has only one photo of herself as a child. When there are no photos, ancestors are forgotten. This film brings one ancestor back: out of a forgotten tin box, restored, named, and hung on the wall of the new home.

- **Place:** Kinshasa, Bandalungwa ("Bandal"). Marty let Claude choose the neighbourhood.
- **Characters:** see `family/chars_b.png`.
  - **The mother, about 35:** pale grey long-sleeved top and a wax-print wrap. She is the granddaughter of Mama Nzeba, and the hands we see in the dusk scenes are hers.
  - **The son, about 8:** mustard-yellow T-shirt.
- **Portrait:** it stays **1972** in its **wax pagne**, as Marty decided. The identity lock applies.
- **Language:** French as spoken in Kinshasa, with Lingala words ("Mbote", "Kobosana te"). English subtitles.
