# Brief — "Jua — More Than a Photograph"

- Track: **Two Minutes to Move** (alt: Animate the Shift — cold restoration workspace → warm lived-in present)
- Arc: **absence → curiosity → belonging**
- Message / end line: **"We can restore the image. We remember the person together."**
- Duration: **75s** target (60–90s window; event max 3 min)
- Aspect: 16:9 (YouTube submission) · Language: testimony in [NATIVE LANGUAGE], subtitles EN/FR
- Thesis: restoration can recover a **face**; only people can recover a **name, a place, a story**. The AI is visible, honest, and never the climax.
- Production: **100% Arcads**, prompted as Jua's pipeline ("Make, restore, and keep the story together" · "Every result explains itself"). See `jua_context.md`.

## The two assets that decide everything (find before generating anything)
| Asset | Needed | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **The photograph** | one real family/community photo, specific place + year: [PLACE], [YEAR] | [ ] |
| **The witness** | one person who can say her name, place, and ONE ordinary true detail (what she made, a phrase she used, where she gathered people) | [ ] |
| Permission | photo owner + witness agree to film use & upload to Arcads (written or recorded "yes") | [ ] |

Have both → documentary mode. Missing either by **Saturday 15:00** → **fictional proof-of-concept mode** (below).

## Honesty rules (non-negotiable)
- The original is shown first, long enough to see what changed, and **last**, side by side with the restoration. The AI version never replaces it.
- Motion is limited to light, fabric, or a camera move. **No speaking, blinking-on-cue, smiling, or invented action** on a real face.
- No AI-generated detail (clothing color, background, place) is presented as fact. If colorized, say so on the end card.
- Name, place, and memory come only from the witness, in their own voice.

## Fictional proof-of-concept mode
- Portrait generated from scratch (no real archival face), testimony voiced by a consenting person reading a written script.
- End card must say: **"Fictional proof of concept. No real person is depicted."**
- Keep a concrete place/year anyway — specificity is what moves people. [FICTIONAL PLACE, YEAR]

## Muted test
Muted, a stranger should still see: worn photo → face becomes clear → someone writes her name on the sleeve. The subtitles carry the testimony. Target: **YES**.

## If the assigned emotion is different
| Emotion | Adjust |
|---|---|
| Belonging (default) | end on the name written + side-by-side |
| Grief | hold on the empty sleeve before the name is written; slower cut |
| Joy | the witness laughs while telling the detail — make that the climax |
| Hope | end on the younger person pinning the photo into a present-day family board |
| Nostalgia | let the remembered phrase repeat over the side-by-side |
