# Official rules — Generathon #2 (source: generathon2final.pdf + generathon.tech/editions/generathon-2)

**This file overrides every other doc in the repo.**

## Our track
**THREE MINUTES TO MOVE — Short Film.** The website calls it "Two Minutes to Move"; both say 3 min max. Pick whichever label the form offers.
- Brief (site): "Suscitez l'émotion choisie en trois minutes maximum" → **the team chooses the emotion.** Ours: **belonging** (absence → curiosity → belonging).
- **Challenge (deck): "Limbic Narration, Not Just a Voice-Over."** → see `brief.md` § Limbic narration.

## Deliverables (submitted on generathon.tech → participant space)
1. **Main video: 3 min max** (the form tolerates up to 4 min — don't use it). Ours: 75s.
2. **Face cam explanation video: 1 min max** (the form says 1:30; follow the stricter 1:00). It **must include all of:** Inspiration · What it is · How you built it · Challenges you ran into · Accomplishments you're proud of · What you learned · What's next · Tools used.

## Judging: 4 × 25%
| Criterion | How Jua scores it |
|---|---|
| Creativity / originality | the AI's limit is the subject: it restores a face, but can't give back a name |
| Execution quality | one room, locked references, consistent face, the silence beat, clean sound mix |
| **AI technical mastery** | multi-model pipeline (gpt-image-2.5 / nano-banana-2 staged restoration, veo31 frozen-face motion, seedance-2.5 with audio references, ElevenLabs/Suno score), identity lock, logged stages shown on screen |
| Emotional impact | limbic narration: breath, silence, touch, a real voice in the room — not an explaining narrator |
